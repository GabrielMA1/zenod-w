What the towel is for

The ZENO Drying Towel is made for the last step of a wash: drying the car once it has been rinsed clean.

Our drying guide explains a careful routine, from checking the towel to the places water collects.

About the 850 GSM figure

GSM is the weight of the fabric per square metre. It describes the material, not how much water a towel holds, so we list it as a specification rather than a performance claim.
