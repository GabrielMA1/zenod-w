About ZENO DETAIL

ZENO DETAIL is a car-care accessories brand for people who look after their own cars.

What we make

We start with the tools you touch at the end of a wash. Our first product is the ZENO Drying Towel: 850 GSM microfiber in blue with black edging, priced for each shopping region.

How we describe products

We publish a specification once it is confirmed, and leave it out until it is. Prices are shown in your shopping region’s currency, and delivery terms are explained before you order. We do not use invented reviews, countdowns or crossed-out prices.

Where we deliver

We deliver across Europe. Delivery within Romania is free; elsewhere, shipping is calculated for the delivery address.
