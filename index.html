The ZENO Drying Towel is made for one job: drying your car after you wash it. It is specified at 850 GSM and finished in blue with black edging.

GSM is the weight of the fabric per square metre. It describes the material, not how much water a towel holds, so we list it as a specification rather than a performance claim.

Drying a car, in four steps

  1. Work in the shade. Dry a cool, fully rinsed car so water does not evaporate into marks.
  2. Check the towel. Make sure it is clean and free of grit before it touches the paint.
  3. Start at the roof. Work downwards with light contact, turning the towel to a dry side as you go.
  4. Finish the edges. Mirrors, handles and panel gaps hold water, so dry them last.
