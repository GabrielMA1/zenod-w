Questions and answers

About the ZENO Drying Towel, prices, delivery and ordering.

The towel

What does 850 GSM mean?

GSM means grams per square metre. The fabric weight specified for this towel is 850 GSM.

What is the ZENO Drying Towel for?

It is designed for drying vehicles after washing.

What is it made from?

The towel has a soft microfiber construction.

What colour is the towel?

It is blue with black edging and ZENO branding.

Prices, delivery and ordering

Can I order now?

Not yet. Online ordering has not opened. You can add the towel to your bag, and it stays saved on this device until ordering opens.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery within Romania is free. For other European addresses, shipping is calculated for the delivery address; there is no single flat rate.

Which currency are prices shown in?

Prices are shown in RON for the Romania shopping region and in EUR for Europe / International. You can change the shopping region at any time under Language and region.

Can I read the site in one language and shop in another region?

Yes. Language and shopping region are separate settings. For example, you can read the site in Romanian and see European prices in EUR.
